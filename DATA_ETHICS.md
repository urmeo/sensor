The data comes from one participant. It includes raw sensor readings and questionnaire responses for Session 1, plus supplied eye summaries for three sessions. The author treats these health-related records as special-category personal data.

Consent records and independent approval evidence are absent from the repository. This page records the author's account; the public files alone do not establish compliance.

## Privacy

- The published schema has no direct identifiers. Anonymous channel numbers do not make the data anonymous.
- Precise clocks, questionnaire answers and physiological patterns can be linked to other information. Keep consent records separate, and avoid re-identification and additional disclosure.
- Copies with relative timestamps remove calendar timestamp columns. They retain sensitive values, and their time origins differ by resource. See the [clock table](data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clocks).
